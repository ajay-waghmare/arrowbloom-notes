# Copyright, lookalike risk, and what makes Arrow Bloom its own game

Written 20 Oct 2026 after Ajay asked: "There won't be any copyright issue? We are using an existing game as
the base." Not legal advice; a working rule set for a solo developer.

## What is and is not protected

- **Game mechanics and rules are not copyrightable.** "Tap an arrow; it leaves the board if nothing is in
  front of it" is an idea, and ideas are free. Easybrain's *Arrow Puzzle* is itself one of many arrow games.
- **What IS protected:** their artwork, icon, sounds, music, level files, store text, screenshots, and
  their name and logo (trademark). Also the overall "look and feel" when copied closely enough that a
  player could mistake one for the other (the *Tetris v. Xio* 2012 ruling turned on copied visual
  expression: grid, piece shapes, colours and layout, not on the rules).
- **Google Play adds its own rules:** impersonation and misleading-metadata policies. An app whose name,
  icon or screenshots make it look like another app can be removed on a complaint without any court.

## Where Arrow Bloom stands

- All code, art (vector icon, neon palette), music (procedural, no files) and text are original and written
  in this repo. Nothing was extracted from any other app.
- The name is **Arrow Bloom**, under the AW EagleCraft "Bloom" family, not "Arrow Puzzle".
  The listing title is "Arrow Bloom – Tap Logic Puzzle": "arrow", "tap" and "puzzle" are descriptive words
  anyone may use; the title never contains another game's full name.
- The listing and screenshots must never reference Easybrain or any other publisher, and must not imitate
  their icon (their arrows on a white rounded square) or their screenshot style.

## What makes it a different game, not a clone

| Arrow Bloom | The arrow games on the store |
|---|---|
| Every escape **blooms**: petals burst, the board plays a note that climbs with the combo | arrows vanish |
| Stars for a **clean** solve, counted in blocked taps; no timer, ever | timers or move limits |
| **Journey through gardens** with their own palettes, then an endless garden | flat level counter |
| **Long arrows** whose whole body must be free | mostly single cells |
| **Daily Bloom**, deterministic and shared, with a streak shield and a spoiler-free share card | rare |
| Procedural **music** that answers every tap; the board is an instrument | stock sound effects |
| Generated and **proven solvable** levels, undo free, hints optional | hand-made levels, ads for hints |
| No ads at launch; later rewarded only, opt-in | interstitials between levels |

Keep building on those; never on their art.
