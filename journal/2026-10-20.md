- **Feel**: procedural music (AudioTrack, pentatonic voices; the note climbs with combo and column), haptics, petal blooms on every escape, hint pulse, shake on a blocked tap. Reduce-motion honours `ANIMATOR_DURATION_SCALE`.

## Decisions
- Level 1 is always fully free: a new player's first tap can never be blocked.
- Stars measure a clean solve (blocked taps), never time. That is the "calm" positioning and it stays.
- 1.0.0 ships with no ads, no analytics, no INTERNET permission; Data safety is "no data collected". Analytics first, then rewarded ads only, each in its own release.
- Package, build dir, Firebase project and AdMob app stay separate from Block Bloom.
- Branches follow Block Bloom: `release/YYYY-MM-DD`; nobody but Ajay merges to `main`.
