**Mechanic (engine rewritten)**
- An arrow is a line of 2+ cells, head first; the cell behind the head is always in line with it.
- Tapping it slides the whole line out along its own path, like a train behind its engine. So an arrow can leave
  exactly when the line from its head to the edge of the board is empty (`CollisionDetector.checkEscape`, head
  ray only; `freeSteps` = how far it slides before it bumps).
- The generator builds boards in reverse: each new head gets an empty line to the edge, the body winds back
  through free cells (straightness, min/max length per garden). Later arrows can sit in front of earlier ones,
  never the reverse, so every board is solvable by construction; the solver re-checks. `PuzzleStats.depth` =
  rounds of "all free arrows leave" = the difficulty chip (Easy <= 3, Normal <= 6, Hard <= 8, Expert beyond).
- Shapes every fifth level (heart, flower, diamond, circle) and a different Daily Bloom shape per day.
- Three hearts per board; a wrong tap bumps back and costs one; stars = hearts kept; at zero hearts the same
  board starts again. Undo and colour streaks removed. Hint = the free arrow most others wait on. # = cell grid.

**Look**: light theme everywhere, per-garden tint and accent, ink arrows, petal bloom at the exit edge, new
adaptive + themed icon (ink L arrow blocked by a green free arrow), light splash, new feature graphic and six
light screenshots with captions (`tools/store-screenshots.js`).
