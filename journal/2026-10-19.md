## Decisions
- **DAG Obstruction Invariant**: Modeled arrow blocking dependencies as a directed graph $G = (V, E)$. Puzzles are guaranteed 100% solvable $\iff G$ is a Directed Acyclic Graph (DAG). Monotonic clearance guarantees greedy solving is optimal with zero backtracking required.
- **Procedural Generation vs Static Level Files**: Generated via deterministic seed algorithms, cutting APK size by several megabytes and enabling infinite levels and daily challenges with zero asset bloat.
- **Build Isolation**: Gradle builds directed to `~/.build-arrowbloom/` to eliminate OneDrive file-lock compilation errors on Windows.
